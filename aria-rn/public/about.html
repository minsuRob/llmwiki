About llmwiki (English)

llmwiki is a personal wiki for keeping what matters from your conversations with AI assistants such as Claude and Codex.

  • Drop: paste a note or link into the inbox without choosing a project or title.
  • Suggestions: an AI assistant suggests linking each note to existing work, grouping notes into new work, or tagging a concept.
  • You decide: you accept or dismiss each suggestion. The AI never confirms or deletes anything on its own.

How llmwiki uses Google data

Google Sign-In is used only to identify your account (email, name, profile photo). Google Drive is accessed with the drive.file scope only: llmwiki can see the "llmwiki 작업실" folder it creates and files you explicitly pick, and nothing else in your Drive. Your work items, notes and suggestions are stored as files you own in that folder. See the Privacy Policy for details.
